# V11.0 — "Everything easier to find"

File: `(V11.0) australia-game-with-ai.tsx`. It is still one self-contained file: a default export, React only. The version label is V11.0 (`GAME_VERSION` 11.0.0). The rules stamp stays 1010 (V10.1's rules), so nothing about how a match plays has changed. New code for the round is in Sections 20BL–20BW, before Section 21; UX-6 (the switch-over) is 20BT–20BW. `V11.0_UX_MAP.md` maps where everything lives, before and after, and is rebuilt by `tools/ux-map/`.

## Changelog for players

* **A new layout, on for everyone.** Find Anything, the layered Settings, What's in the game and the side panel are now on by default.
  * If you played before, the game moves you to the new layout once. It says so on the main menu and names the switch that brings the V10.1 look back.
  * After that, your own choices stick. Turn any of the four off and it stays off.
* **🔎 Find Anything.** One search box covers every action, screen, setting, keyboard shortcut and LAB card.
  * Press 🔎 Find or Ctrl/⌘+K, type a few letters and press Enter. The result opens through the same button you would have pressed, and a setting opens at its own row.
  * Type a question and Ask the Game AI answers it.
  * Everyday words work: "dock" finds the side panel, and "classic" finds the classic switch.
* **⭐ Essentials.** Settings opens on the 12 choices most players want, plus the classic switch.
  * Everything else is filed under five goals instead of 11 tabs: Match & rules, Your rival, Teams & automation, Help, screen & sound, and Records & tools.
  * Every setting has one home, and the search still finds every one.
* **🧩 What's in the game.** One page has a card for every optional feature. Each card says what the feature does, whether it is on, what it needs and where it lives in Settings, and carries the feature's real switch.
* **📑 Side panel.** On screens at least 1024 px wide, Market (Sell and Buy), Challenges, Travel, Workshop and Investments open as tabs beside the map, so the map and End Turn stay in reach. Floating buttons, the Widget Dock and the classic quick bar move aside to make room.
* **Prefer how V10.1 looked?** Turn on **🕰️ Classic layout (V10.1)**.
  * It is on the main menu, in ⭐ Essentials, in Find Anything (type "classic"), and in the classic view's Settings › Interface & Accessibility.
  * While it is on, the game looks and behaves as V10.1.
  * Your choices for the four new screens are kept and come back when you turn it off.
* **One look for the new screens.** Find Anything, Essentials, What's in the game and the side panel share the same spacing, type sizes, buttons and a clear keyboard focus ring. They are readable in light, dark and Retro themes, at the largest text size and with reduced motion.
* **Small fixes.**
  * Negotiations lists each of its options once.
  * "Travel Discipline Strength" and "Floating UI Conflict Control Options" are now named apart from their switches; the classic layout keeps the old titles.
  * A setting opened from a search lands on its own row.
* **Your matches are safe.** Saves and replays from V9.7, V9.8, V10.0 and V10.1 load and play move for move.

## Technical note

**Compatibility.** Nothing changed in the rules, the RNG or the save format.
* The rules stamp stays 1010.
* UX-2's save rung (`migrateSaveTo1100`) changes nothing.
* The five V11.0 switches are profile settings. A save never carries them and a load never restores them.
* Real V9.7, V9.8, V10.0 and V10.1 saves give the V10.1 build's own results, both headless (`v116so_39`: five saves, three days, with all four switches on, all off, as saved and in classic: 20 of 20) and in Chromium (10 of 10 traces).

**The switch-over** (SECTION 20BU).
* The four switches now default to on: `findAnythingEnabled`, `settingsLayersEnabled`, `whatsInTheGameEnabled` and `dockedPanelEnabled`. No other default changed: V10.1's 599 defaults keep their fingerprint, and the one new key, `classicLayoutV101`, defaults to false.
* At start-up, `v116BootSwitchOver` reads one localStorage record, `aussie.v110.layoutSwitchOver` (`{version: 1, status: 'new' | 'moved', noticeSeen}`). There are three cases:
  * **No record, and the browser holds a first-run record or stored preferences** (a returning player). The four switches are set on once, the record says `moved`, and the main menu shows the notice. The notice is marked seen as soon as the menu draws it.
  * **No record and neither** (a new player). Nothing moves, and the record says `new`.
  * **A record.** Nothing moves again.
* Blocked or broken storage never throws. A profile whose storage is blocked starts as new, with no notice.

**The classic switch.**
* `classicLayoutV101` is a profile setting: remembered between visits, never in a save.
* It is read inside the four predicates (`v110FindAnythingOn`, `v113LayersOn`, `v114PageOn` and `v115DockOn`), the single places that gate the V11.0 screens, so all four go off together.
* It never writes the four switches' values.
* Turning it on or off closes any open V11.0 window and says where the switch is.
* The self-tests check that classic hands back V10.1's very objects: the surface tables, the shortcut list, the Find Anything results, Ctrl/⌘+K and the hub.

**One look** (SECTION 20BT).
* `V116_UI` is one small set of shared styles: type sizes, spacing, buttons, chips, fields, the selected mark and a keyboard-only focus ring (sky-600, 2 px, offset 2 px). It is used by the four V11.0 screens only.
* The game's global focus rules (SECTION 20P) still win when high-contrast focus rings are on, which is the default: a yellow ring with a dark halo.
* Text sizes are in rem, or the 11 px the Text size setting scales.
* White text on green uses green-700 (`v116OnClass`), and counts on coloured buttons are drawn at full strength. Both read 4.5:1 or better.
* The side panel's card is drawn solid (`v116SolidCard`). Retro's card is 90% opaque, and the panel has no dimmed backdrop behind it, so the play screen showed through. With a solid card, the old windows' text in the panel reads as it does in its own window.

**Clean-ups** (SECTION 20BV).
* **(a) Negotiations.** In the layered view, the moved "more negotiation options" block leaves out the three options the Negotiations section already draws.
* **(b) Room for the panel.** While the side panel is open, its style element moves the Ask and Guardian buttons, the Widget Dock and the notification lane to the panel's left. It also centres the classic layout's quick bar and turn pill on the page beside the panel. A widget the player placed by hand keeps its place.
* **(c) "dock"** finds the side panel.
* **(d) The exact row.** Find Anything and the settings search open a setting at its row (`v116FindRow` and `v116LocateRowInPage`).
* **(e) Lint suppressions.** `useV115DockedPanel` now reads its latest props through a ref and lists every dependency, so its two `eslint-disable` comments are gone (eslint-plugin-react-hooks: 0 warnings).

**Judgement calls you should know about**
1. **Who counts as returning:** a browser with a first-run record or any stored preference. The game writes a first-run record when the guided first match is finished, skipped or switched off, or when a save is loaded. A browser with neither counts as new, and the new layout is its default anyway.
2. **The notice** is marked seen when the main menu draws it, not when it is dismissed, so a reload never shows it twice. Until the player closes it, it stays on the menu for that visit.
3. **Item 4** was done for the twins only, as you chose. Other rows drawn from old sections keep their sections' titles.
4. **The restyle** covers the four V11.0 screens only. The map's §1 said "UX-6 restyles everything"; I followed the brief.
5. **While classic is on,** the Settings view switcher leaves out the two test ids the V11.0 build added, so classic matches V10.1 element for element.
6. **What's New** draws one V11.0 entry covering the whole round. UX-2's `V110_CHANGELOG` stays in the file.

**Known gaps**
* A setting whose row only draws when its parent switch is on, or inside the closed Teams group, lands on its section or on the Teams gate, not on the row. In the browser walk of all 604 settings in a solo match, Enter opened 182 at their row and 15 at their ⭐ Essentials choice. It opened 183 at their section and 223 at the Teams gate (the Teams group is closed in a solo match); the same walk on the UX-5 build (the four switches on) opened 69 at their row, 292 at their section, and 243 with nothing marked, among them all 223 in the closed Teams group and the 15 in Essentials.
* A widget the player dragged, or Floating UI Conflict Control relocated, is not moved beside the panel.
* V10.1's own Guardian and Ask buttons overlap each other by 9 px. This is unchanged.
* Layered Settings draws about twice as many elements as the classic Simple view, so it opens a little slower (see the times below).
* The in-game LAB runner's numbers were not re-measured; the node runner was.

**Risks**
* Every returning player gets the new layout at once, so expect "where did X go?". Find Anything and the classic switch are the answers, and the notice names both.
* The beside-panel rules find the widgets by their classes and labels. If one of those widgets changes its classes later, it quietly stops moving. A self-test pins the selectors.
* The exact-row finder matches a row by its drawn title. A row renamed later falls back to its section, as before.
* Classic was compared with the V10.1 build text for text in the six windows and every Settings tab, not pixel for pixel.

**Opening times.** Measured from the key press to the next frame after the screen is drawn, in the same loaded Day 4 match at 1280×900. The production build was used (React production mode, the way the game is played), median of 15 runs:

| Build | Settings | Find Anything | What's in the game |
|---|---|---|---|
| V10.1 build | 31.4 ms | — | — |
| Before UX-6, its default (the four off) | 20.6 ms | — | — |
| Before UX-6, the four on | 32.4 ms | 24.8 ms | 63.7 ms |
| After UX-6, the default (new layout) | 37.4 ms | 25.7 ms | 49.2 ms |
| After UX-6, classic layout on | 27.3 ms | — | — |

Settings in the layered view opens about 5 ms slower than before UX-6 with the four on (an earlier quiet run of 15 gave 31.3 → 36.9 ms). Find Anything is unchanged (21.7 → 22.4 ms in that run). What's in the game moves by up to 15 ms between runs, so its difference is noise (48.0 → 57.1 ms in that run, 63.7 → 49.2 ms in this one). Classic opens Settings in V10.1's time. The dev build (React dev mode, served by Vite) is 14 to 34 times slower on every screen, so its medians of 7, given in the map's §20, mostly measure React's dev checks.

**Test by hand**
1. **Returning player.** In a browser that has played before, open V11.0. The notice shows once on the main menu and names 🕰️ Classic layout (V10.1). Reload, and it is gone. To see it again, delete the `aussie.v110.layoutSwitchOver` entry from the site's local storage and reload.
2. **Classic.** Turn on 🕰️ Classic layout from the menu.
   * 🔎 Find, 🧩 What's in the game and the side panel disappear.
   * Settings shows the 11 tabs with V10.1's titles, and Ctrl+K does what it did in V10.1.
   * Turn it off, and the four come back as you left them.
3. **Find Anything.** Press Ctrl/⌘+K and type "dock", then "classic". The first result opens the side panel, then ⭐ Essentials at the classic switch's row. In a Team Mode match, "Travel Discipline Strength" opens Settings at that slider's row; in a solo match it opens the Teams gate, because the Teams group is closed there.
4. **The side panel at 1280 px.** Open a match with Ask the Game AI on and press C. The Ask button and the Widget Dock move left of the panel and come back when you press Escape. With the classic play-screen layout, the quick bar centres beside the panel.
5. **Keyboard and look.** Use the largest text size and the light theme.
   * Tab to 🔎 Find, then open Settings and What's in the game.
   * Close each with Escape.
   * Check that the ring is visible on every focused control.
